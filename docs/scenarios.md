# Scenarios

Files live in `packages/sim/scenarios/*.json`, generated by `packages/sim/scripts/generate-scenarios.ts`
(`pnpm --filter @bathroombestie/sim generate`). Times are minutes from the contract's window start. Scenario phones report every `DEFAULT_REPORT_EVERY_MIN` (3) minutes, the same cadence the app uses (`thresholds.report_every_min`), so alert times land on or just after a report tick.
`truth` feeds the sweep: `normal` nights count false alerts, `concerning` nights count time-to-alert and misses,
`excluded` nights are ignored by the sweep.

| #   | Name                     | Truth      | Expectation (standard preset)                                                                                              |
| --- | ------------------------ | ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | normal-night             | normal     | no alert (bar change, 20 min queue, home geofence)                                                                         |
| 2   | battery-dies             | normal     | no alert, soft note only                                                                                                   |
| 3   | separated-then-silent    | concerning | rung 2 within [162,170]; onset 140                                                                                         |
| 4   | false-alarm-confirmed    | normal     | rung 2 in [123,127], max rung 2, resolved by confirm                                                                       |
| 5   | worry-press              | excluded   | rung 2 at the minute of the press, despite normal evidence                                                                 |
| 6   | readiness-blocked        | excluded   | preflight `not_ready` (activation would return 409)                                                                        |
| 7   | brief-separation         | normal     | no alert (20 min apart, then rejoins)                                                                                      |
| 8   | separated-only           | normal     | rung-1 nudge at 127 only (first apart report is on a 3-min tick), closed as `rejoined`                                     |
| 9   | home-on-time             | normal     | no alert (leaves at 215, home at 230, deadline 240)                                                                        |
| 10  | home-late-reporting      | normal     | rung-1 nudge at 251 only (deadline 240 + 10 grace), closed by arriving home at 300                                         |
| 11  | home-missed-and-quiet    | concerning | nudge at 251, hard at 254 (silent 20 min), rung 2 at 259; last seen _with_ the group, so only the milestone catches it     |
| 12  | exit-alone-arrives       | normal     | rung-1 separation nudge at 175 only; home at 189 (39 min). At cautious (30 min) the exit-alone clock alerts a friend first |

Scenario 4's confirm is hand-timed for the standard preset. At relaxed the separation nudge is already open when the
confirm lands, so it closes early and nothing reaches a friend; at cautious the ladder has already climbed to rung 3.
Those false-alert numbers in the sweep are properties of this one scenario's timing, not of the presets. Add scenarios
with confirms timed per preset before trusting the false-alert axis.
